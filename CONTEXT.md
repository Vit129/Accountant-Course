# Accountant Learning — Domain Language

Interactive study portal for Thailand's Certified Public Accountant (CPA) examination.

## Language

**Track**:
A dedicated syllabus for one of the CPA preparation subjects (Financial Accounting 1 & 2, Auditing 1 & 2, Law, Taxation, Cost Accounting, Financial Management).
_Avoid_: Subject folder, Class, Course module

**Lesson**:
A discrete learning unit containing accounting/auditing theory, practice problem, entry template, and automated answer verification.
_Avoid_: Chapter, Lecture, Assignment

**Capstone**:
The single continuous multi-step case study (`Final-Project`) integrating all 8 subjects into one sequential company audit scenario.
_Avoid_: Independent quiz, Track 9, General test

**Mock Exam**:
A timed mixed examination randomly drawing questions across tracks 1–8 to simulate official TFAC exam conditions.
_Avoid_: Quiz, Flashcards, Test bank

**Adjusting Journal Entry (AJE)**:
An accounting adjustment proposed by an auditor to correct unrecorded or misstated transactions before closing financial statements.
_Avoid_: Correction, Ledger fix

**Daily Check-in**:
Streak tracking system that increments only after the learner successfully passes at least one lesson on that calendar day.
_Avoid_: Login streak, Daily bonus

**Milestone**:
Progress recognition awarded at 50%, 75%, and 100% subject completion, generating a downloadable completion certificate at 100%.
_Avoid_: Badge, Level-up
